# Abstract

BACKGROUND:\ Thermal injuries to the skin (burns) are common household and man-made traumatic injuries in humans. In wartime, thermal injuries are additional damaging factors in combined skin lesions caused by the damaging elements of modern weapons. AIM:\ This study aimed to evaluate the effectiveness of gels based on silver nanoparticles in combination with humic acid on the healing of thermal skin injuries (burn wounds) in rats. MATERIALS AND METHODS:\ In the experiments on 57 white nonlinear male rats weighing 218–260 g, the wound healing effects of gels with 10% humic acid modified with nanosilver on various bases, prepared in a pharmacopoeial manner, on a grade IIIB thermal skin burn model were examined.
